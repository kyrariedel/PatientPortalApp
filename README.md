# Patient Portal Application - BridgeCare

### Slogan:
> "Connecting patients and physicians for better care."

### App Description:
BridgeCare is an app that helps physicians manage patient recovery by assigning tasks, tracking progress with updates and photos, and reviewing healing status in real-time. 
It simplifies follow-up appointments and makes sharing information with family members or caregivers easy, offering seamless care and clear progress at every step. 
Designed for physicians, patients, caregivers, and healthcare clinics, it’s perfect for coordinating patient care efficiently.

### App Features:
- Physicians
- Patients
- Tasks
- Appointments
- Photos
- Realtime Firebase Database
